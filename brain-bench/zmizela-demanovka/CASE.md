# Case "Zmizelá Demänovka" — author's case bible (SPOILERS)

Design document for the Brain Bench detective game. Player-facing text is Czech; this file is English.
Players: the whole family (13–17 year-olds plus adults), main device (timer, hints, accusation form) + a second device in reader mode. Target: 25 minutes, ~14 documents, ~1 500 words of player text.
Framing: "the file from the investigation team" (a recent case). The team is the two oldest girls, Tereza and Žofie, who compiled the file on Sunday morning; the player is the detective they hand it to.
Tone: light, no violence. Every person is a real family member; every place except the village of Strážné and the Krkonoše mountains is fictional (chata Jeřabinka, krmelec, vyhlídka Na Křížku). No adult ages are stated (real people); children's ages are invented.

## The weekend and the setting

- Friday 30 January – Sunday 1 February 2026. Chata Jeřabinka, about 3 km above Strážné, Krkonoše National Park. Cars stay at the car park in Strážné; luggage goes up and down with the cabin's snow groomer (rolba), which did not run on Saturday or that night. Walking Strážné ↔ cabin: 55 min up, 40 min down.
- Phones have no mobile signal at or around the cabin; everyone uses the cabin Wi-Fi (LTE router with a roof antenna, on the 1st-floor corridor). Wi-Fi covers the whole cabin including the porch and ends about 15 m outside the front door. A device in range stays connected with the screen off. Photos taken out of range upload to the shared album only when the phone reconnects.
- **Floors** (one staircase links all three; the attic is reached only via the 1st-floor corridor):
  - Ground floor: porch (zádveří, unheated, shelf used as a cold store: drinks, milk, vegetable crate, Ludmila's bábovka; jackets and boots; weather-station display; the front door is the only way out — the terrace door is snowed in), hall with the staircase, dining room with kitchen corner (solid wooden door to the hall, kept shut: from the table you cannot see the hall, stairs or porch), bedroom of **Konečných**, shower room with toilet.
  - 1st floor: two bedrooms of **Šimečkovi**, the only bathroom with a **bathtub** (and toilet), corridor with the router. The bathroom has a turn lock that can be turned **from outside with a coin** (emergency release).
  - Attic: **Žákovi** (two rooms), no bathroom.
- Outside: the only path uphill goes from the front door past the deer feeding rack (krmelec) with Ctirad's wildlife camera (fotopast) — 5 min up / 4 min down — and on to the viewpoint Na Křížku with the only wooden cross around — 8 min up / 6 min down — where it ends. Anyone going to the cross passes the camera. Walking times are approximate (±1 min).
- Weather station: dry all Saturday evening; snow 22:20–05:50; fresh snow 32 cm (61 → 93 cm).
- The bottle: Demänovka 0.7 l, unopened, brought by Konečných for the Sunday farewell toast; Ctirad tied a small carved wooden spruce on a string around its neck (distinctive feature). The team counted all bottles: only this one is missing.

## Why nobody worked it out at breakfast
Three things went missing the same night and two people (Petr, Štěpán) were caught hiding night trips, so everyone chased night stories. The walk organiser (Michaela) looked like the "surprise" suspect. Alena's bath looked confirmed: Michaela saw her in a bathrobe and Alžběta found the bathroom locked with the light on.

## Solution chain

1. **When the bottle left:** Tomáš saw it at 21:15 (N). Camera 21:36 shows a bottle carried up (G); the album photo 21:46 shows the bottle with the wooden spruce at the cross (H), the spruce is the Demänovka's feature (A). So it left the cabin between 21:15 and 21:36 (answer "21:15–21:40").
2. **Whose phone photographed the cross at 21:46:** the album (H) shows no device (shared family account), only shot 21:46 / upload 21:57. Router (F): the device that came back on the Wi-Fi at 21:57 is iPhone-13 (decisive pair for the model: F + H). Chat (E): iPhone 13 = Alena. Alena (I): phone with her all the time, never lent. Red herring in the same window: Ctirad's Galaxy-A15 off 21:30–21:42 because Ludmila confiscated and switched it off (he was watching hockey under the table) — score-sheet note (D) and his interview (L); he never left the table.
3. **Alena's alibi collapses:** she claims she streamed an audiobook over Wi-Fi in the bath; router (F): iPhone-13 off the Wi-Fi 21:31–21:57; the locked bathroom proves nothing because the lock turns from outside with a coin (B); the camera shows someone going up at 21:36 and down at 21:53 (G), consistent with the map (C).
4. **Everyone else covered 21:15–21:57:** score sheet (D) with round times and Ludmila's + team's note that from 21:22 to 22:05 Petr, Míša, Ctirad, Tereza, Žofie and Ludmila never left the table; 21:15–21:22 break was at the table except Tomáš's beer run and Alena leaving at 21:20; Tomáš in view; Kateřina + Alžběta (N) keep Štěpán in the attic until 22:03.
5. **Where:** the photo shows it at the cross and the card says "Hledejte u kříže" (H); only one path leads there and it passes the camera; after 21:53 only Štěpán (at the rack, 22:08–22:12) and Žofie (08:36) passed the camera (G); 32 cm of snow fell after 22:20 (A) → buried at the cross. The team searched the whole cabin incl. shelf, jackets and luggage (A); Žofie found only hay and carrots in the rack at 08:36 (G); the car is 40 min away (C).
6. **Why:** the card (H) — farewell gift "from Krakonoš", bottle for the adults' toast, gingerbread for the children, "look by the cross" — and the 10:00 walk to the cross (A) → a surprise for the Sunday walk.
7. **Red herrings:** Petr — 23:52, after the bottle left, cake in hand (J, H); Štěpán — 22:03–22:16, carrots (N, F, G); Ctirad — 06:30, shelf already empty at 23:52 (J); Michaela — afternoon trip while the bottle was still home, at the table during the act (D).
