# Cold case "Mánesova 41" — author's case bible (SPOILERS)

Design document for the Brain Bench detective game. Player-facing text is Czech; this file is English.
Players: two 15-year-olds, one tablet (main: timer, hints, accusation form) and one phone (reader mode). Target: 45 minutes.

## The crime (what really happened)

Tuesday 14 November 2006, Prague 2 – Vinohrady, antique shop "Starožitnosti Hron", Mánesova 41 (ground floor, courtyard back door).

- Victim: **Karel Hron** (64), antiquarian, widower. Every Tuesday he closes the showroom at 18:30, arms the alarm zone "Prodejna" (showroom) and stays in the back office doing the books until about 21:00.
- Killer: **Viktor Lang** (38), alarm technician of the firm Alarmservis Praha, the only technician assigned to this shop. He is deep in debt (enforcement order for 248 000 Kč, due 30 Nov 2006).
- Plan: on Monday 13 Nov Lang made an unannounced "routine check" of the alarm (service report says "bez závad"). Karel mentioned that he works late on Tuesdays. Lang said he might drop by on Tuesday evening to "adjust a sensor".
- 19:45 Lang rings at the courtyard door. Karel knows him and lets him in.
- 19:52 Lang disarms the showroom zone with the **service code 00** (Karel never knew or used that code; he always used user code 1).
- About 19:55 Karel catches Lang at the showroom vitrine; Lang strikes him with a bronze candlestick. The upstairs neighbour hears a heavy thump during the TV weather forecast.
- Lang takes the garnet collection from the vitrine and opens the safe, whose code Karel kept on a card in the desk drawer (Eva confirms Karel "wrote things down").
- 20:38 Lang re-arms the showroom with the service code, so the alarm looks normal, and leaves through the courtyard. Neighbour sees a man with a **reflective stripe across the back of his jacket** carrying a sports bag at about 20:40. It is raining (rain started about 20:10).
- 20:51 Lang buys coffee and cigarettes at the Benzina station, Vinohradská 120 (300 m away), and pays by card ending **4417**. Back at the courtyard to fetch his van, he throws the receipt into the courtyard bin.
- 21:35 He gets home to Zbraslav. His wife covers for him in her statement ("home all evening") but lets slip that his jacket was soaking wet and that he said he had "taken the bins out".
- Wednesday 15 Nov 08:30 assistant **Eva Malá** finds the body.

## Why the 2006 investigation failed

- They focused on the son (debts, inheritance) and the ex-partner (lawsuit); both had alibis.
- The alarm company said the service code is known to all six of its technicians, so the log was not treated as decisive.
- Lang's wife gave him an alibi.
- The courtyard bin was inventoried, but the petrol receipt was never matched with Lang's bank statement, which sits in the file only because police checked suspects' finances.
- The case was shelved on 30 June 2007.

## Solution chain (what the players must connect)

1. **Who could open the alarm:** alarm log (E) shows disarm/re-arm with service code 00 at 19:52 and 20:38, while Karel always used code 1 (log and Eva). Alarmservis letter (F): only Lang services this shop and the deputy was in Egypt.
2. **Lang lies about being at home:** petrol receipt from the courtyard bin (O, 20:51, card ****4417) matches his bank statement (P: 14.11 Benzina Vinohradská, ****4417). The wife's slip (wet jacket; it started raining at 20:10 according to the weather record) fits.
3. **The neighbour's description** (reflective stripe, sports bag) matches the Alarmservis leaflet photo (technicians' jackets) and the pretext visit on 13 Nov (service report, Eva).
4. **Motive:** enforcement order 248 000 Kč due 30 Nov 2006.
5. **Eliminations:** Tomáš (photo 20:31 at the theatre vs re-arm 20:38), Šimek (Brno, lot 112 at 20:05), Eva (choir + she would have used code 2), Petra (with Tomáš).

Accusation form: culprit (one of 6), how he got in and opened the alarm (4 options), motive (4 options), and the two pieces of evidence that break his alibi (pick from the document list). Correct: Lang / let in by Karel + service code / debts / receipt + bank statement.
